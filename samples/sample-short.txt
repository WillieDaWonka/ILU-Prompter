## Welcome

Good evening, and welcome to the library's open evening.

My name is Sam, and I will be your host for the next ten minutes.

Tonight you can explore three things. The reading room, where the quiet is free. The workshop corner, where the noise is allowed. And the tea table, where the biscuits are strictly first come, first served.

## What is happening tonight

At half past seven, we will begin a short tour of the old map collection. Please meet at the front desk.

At eight o'clock, there is a story hour for families in the children's room. Pyjamas are encouraged, but not required.

At half past eight, our local writers will read a few pages from their new books. There will be time for questions, and for one very small prize for the best question.

## Thank you

That is all from me.

Please look after one another, ask the staff if you cannot find something, and remember that every book here is yours to borrow.

Enjoy your evening, and thank you for coming.
